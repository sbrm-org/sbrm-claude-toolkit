# Nonprofit Management Cheat Sheet (Operations Lens)

## Foundations
- **Purpose stack**: mission → strategy → portfolio → programs → projects → tasks → habits
- **Priority order**: client outcomes & safety; cash & solvency; staff capacity & health; governance alignment; speed of learning
- **Resource engine** (social sector): money (unrestricted/restricted), time (staff/volunteers), brand/goodwill

## Organizational Health & Clarity (*The Advantage*)
### Six Questions
Answer and repeat until boring:
1. Why we exist
2. How we behave
3. What we do
4. How we succeed
5. What's most important now (single thematic goal, 6-12 weeks)
6. Who must do what

- **Daily check-in**: 5-10 minutes
- **Weekly tactical**: 60-90 minutes
- **Monthly strategic**: 2-3 topics
- **Quarterly off-site**: team health + strategy

### Cascade Clarity
Leaders communicate the six answers until boring

## Portfolio & Sustainability (*Nonprofit Sustainability*)
### Matrix Map
- **X-axis**: Mission impact (1-5 score)
- **Y-axis**: Full-cost profitability
- **Bubble size**: Expense/scale

### Impact Rubric (score 1-5 each; average)
- Depth of outcome
- Reach
- Quality
- Equity/justice
- Strategic fit
- Learning potential

### Full-Cost Method
Direct + fair share of shared costs
- **Drivers**: time, sq ft, transactions
- **Step-down** allocation if needed

### Quadrants & Default Strategies
- **Stars** (high impact, profitable): invest/grow
- **Hearts** (high impact, unprofitable): protect; manage subsidy; improve efficiency; find funding
- **Money Trees** (lower impact, profitable): maintain/milk within mission guardrails
- **Stop Signs** (lower impact, unprofitable): fix fast, partner, or exit

### Subsidy Management
- Intentional subsidy: name source, size, duration
- Review annually

## Impact Scoring Rubric
Score 1-5 each (define anchors for 1 and 5):
- Depth of outcome
- Reach/scale
- Quality/excellence
- Equity/justice
- Strategic fit/learning potential

### Example Anchors for "Depth of Outcome"
- **1**: Temporary relief with no sustained change in well-being (e.g., single meal, emergency shelter night with no follow-up)
- **3**: Modest improvement with some durability (e.g., job training completion, short-term stabilization with case management)
- **5**: Transformative, durable improvement in multiple domains of well-being (e.g., permanent housing + sustained employment + health stability; family reunification with ongoing support)

## Full-Cost Allocation (Quick Guide)
- **Choose sensible drivers**: time (labor), square feet (facilities), transactions (admin), users/clients (shared tech)
- **Step-down allocation** for support departments before program rollups
- **Sanity checks**: cost shares roughly mirror effort/usage

## Finance Mini-Formulas & Triggers
- **Days Cash on Hand target**: 60-180 days (set by risk/volatility)
- **Revenue dependency trigger**: single funder >20% or high concentration—document mitigation
- **Subsidy cap**: define max % of unrestricted for subsidies; review annually
- **Cash triggers**: if forecast <8 weeks, enact contingency plan (spend freeze, collections push, line draw, board bridge)

## Measurement & Decision Science (*How to Measure Anything*)
- **Measure to reduce uncertainty** that matters to a decision; if no decision changes, don't measure
- **VOI** (value of information): prioritize what to measure; stop when VOI < cost
- **Calibrated judgment**: 90% confidence ranges that hit ~90% of the time
- **Small samples help**: random samples of 5-12 can shrink uncertainty
- **Bayesian updating**: start with a prior; update with new data
- **Decision model skeleton**: decisions → outcomes → uncertain variables → distributions → sensitivity → VOI

## Learning & Measurement Plan (VOI Pattern)
For each risky assumption:
- Smallest test ≤60 days
- Metric & success threshold
- Predeclared action per result
- Stop when the likely decision won't change

## Evaluation Design (*Impact Evaluation in Practice*)
Match design to decision, feasibility, and ethics:
- **Implementation**: process study/qualitative
- **Modest attribution**: pre-post with trends
- **Plausible counterfactual**: diff-in-diff; matched comparison
- **Strong causal claim**: RCT
- **Threshold-based**: regression discontinuity

**Ethics**: informed consent where appropriate; low-risk tests; protect vulnerable groups

## Innovation & Scale (*Lean Impact*)
- **Think Big, Start Small, Learn Fast**
- **Minimum Viable Intervention (MVI)** to test riskiest assumption first
- **Innovation accounting**: hypothesis → metric & threshold → timebox → decision (pivot/persevere/stop)
- **Partner for scale**: align funders/implementers who accept iteration

## Org Lifecycles (*Nonprofit Lifecycles*)
### Idea/Start-Up
- <3 years old, budget <$500k, founder-led, pilot programs, informal systems, hand-to-mouth fundraising
- **Capacity focus**: establish mission/vision; recruit initial board; pilot program design; basic bookkeeping; build credibility

### Growth
- 3-10 years, $500k-$3M budget, rapid program expansion, adding staff, formalizing systems, diversifying revenue
- **Capacity focus**: hire professional staff (finance, HR, development); document policies and procedures; implement CRM/database; strategic plan; build cash reserves

### Mature
- >10 years, >$3M budget, stable established programs, deep infrastructure, recognized brand, succession planning for founder/long-tenured leaders
- **Capacity focus**: optimize program portfolio (Matrix Map); deepen outcome measurement and evaluation; build endowment or significant reserves; invest in leadership pipeline; refresh strategy to avoid stagnation

### Decline or Turnaround
- Any age/size; symptoms include declining revenue, mission drift or loss of focus, leadership crisis or high turnover, reputational damage, deteriorating finances (reserves depleted, mounting debt)
- **Capacity focus**: refocus mission and core programs; exit weak/off-mission programs; rebuild cash and credibility; stabilize leadership; engage board actively; consider merger/dissolution if turnaround not viable

## Crucial Conversations Toolkit
### Keep Dialogue Safe
- Mutual purpose/respect

### STATE
- Share facts
- Tell story
- Ask for others'
- Talk tentatively
- Encourage testing

### AMPP
- Ask
- Mirror
- Paraphrase
- Prime

### Move to Action
- Decide who decides
- Document who/what/when
- Follow up

### Power-Dynamics Caveat
STATE and AMPP techniques assume roughly equal power and psychological safety. In supervisor-staff, ED-board, or funder-grantee conversations, the lower-power party may need explicit process agreements, third-party facilitation, or advocacy support to make dialogue truly safe.

## Strategic Planning
### Planning Cycle
- Typically 3-5 year horizon
- Annual review and tactical refresh
- Major revision when environment or leadership shifts significantly

### Planning Process
- Stakeholder input (staff, board, clients/beneficiaries, community partners, funders)
- Environmental scan (SWOT, PESTLE, trends)
- Theory of change development
- Goals with SMART targets
- Assign owners and timelines
- Board approval

### Theory of Change
If [inputs/resources] support [activities], then [outputs] lead to [short-term outcomes] → [intermediate outcomes] → [long-term impact], because [assumptions]. Test assumptions with data.

### Strategic Goals
- Typically 3-5 enterprise-level goals (not laundry list)
- Each with 2-4 year targets
- Translated into annual objectives

### Implementation Tracking
- Quarterly progress reviews with leadership team
- Adjust tactics as needed
- Report to board with stoplight status (green/yellow/red)
- Annual retreat to assess and refresh

### Environmental Scanning
- Track sector trends, policy changes, funder priorities, competitor/collaborator moves, demographic shifts
- Update quarterly
